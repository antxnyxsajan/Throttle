// GENERAL GUIDELINES

HACKATHON RULES

Official guidelines and requirements applicable to all participating teams.

RULE 01

Team Size

Teams must consist of 3–5 members. Cross-team collaboration on code during the event is strictly prohibited.

RULE 02

Deliverables

A working prototype (not just a slide deck), a 3–5 minute live demo, and a one-page technical write-up explaining your architecture, trade-offs, and mechanism design.

RULE 03

Open Tech Stack

Completely open. Use whatever programming languages, frameworks, or open-source libraries you can defend during the live demo.

RULE 04

Chaos Injection Clause

Judges reserve the right to inject standardized chaos scenarios into your live demo (e.g., GPS dropouts, bursts of adjournments, or sudden solar crashes) to test resilience.

RULE 05

AI Tool Usage

AI assistants are permitted, but submissions are judged on the quality and depth of product logic, state management, and constraint satisfaction. Thin prompt wrappers will be disqualified.

RULE 06

Original Code & Demo

No pre-built solutions. Repositories created before the event start time will be disqualified. Each team gets 5 minutes (3 min demo + 2 min Q&A) on their own machine.

JUDGING CRITERIA

Each problem statement includes domain-specific criteria (viewable in the problem details). General weightage focuses on:

25%

Technical Execution

Architectural robustness, code cleanliness, and stability under streaming or accelerated data.

25%

Problem Solving

Accuracy of business logic, optimization under hard constraints, and novelty of approach.

20%

UI / UX Polish

Clarity of operations dashboards, responsive layouts, and intuitive user workflows.

20%

Real-World Feasibility

Practical viability under field conditions, edge-case resilience, and chaos tolerance.

10%

Live Demo & Q&A

Clarity of live demonstration on your own machine and confident defense during jury Q&A.

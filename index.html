>_ INTRODUCTION

WHO ARE WE?

Hack Club RSET is a student-led tech community of RSET — empowering students to learn, build and innovate through technology.

Focused on hands-on learning and open collaboration — exploring coding, cybersecurity, AI and emerging technologies through hackathons, workshops, projects and community-driven events.

>_ THE EVENT

WHAT IS THROTTLE

THROTTLE is a 16-hour overnight tech experience where innovation meets collaboration through hackathons, CTFs and other challenges.

Participants spend 16 hours building innovative projects, solving challenges, exploring emerging technologies, and connecting with like-minded innovators.
